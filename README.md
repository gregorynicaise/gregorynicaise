### 🧬 About

French research scientist specialised in experimental and computational science applied to nuclear safety analysis. Chemical engineering degree, MSc and PhD in materials science, statistical physics and quantum mechanics. 

Currently serving as **Scientific Advisor at the ASNR Nuclear Emergency Centre**, leading the development and implementation of advanced methods and fast computational tools for real-time predictive evaluation of accident source terms, and heading the emergency source term response team.


### 🔬 Areas of expertise

- Accident source term evaluation & predictive computation 
- Fission product physical chemistry & thermochemistry
- Severe nuclear accident physics
- Safety review of innovative reactor concepts 
- Deep geological disposal safety review
- Nuclear emergency preparedness and response
